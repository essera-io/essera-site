How an engagement works

From the problem to software you own.

  1. Find the cost

    We sit with your team, trace the process end to end, and identify where time, errors and money are being lost.

  2. Scope the fix

    A short written plan: what we'll build, what it should save, how long it takes and what it costs.

  3. Build and ship

    We build in small increments against your real systems and data, with your team reviewing as we go.

  4. Hand over

    You own the code, the documentation and the knowledge. We stay available for support and the next problem.

Questions

Common questions from prospective clients.

What does a project cost?

We price each project after scoping it. Before any work starts, you get a short written plan with what we will build, what it should save and a fixed cost.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the problem. Scoping takes days, not weeks, and we build in small increments so you see working software early instead of waiting until the end.

Do we own the code?

Yes. You own the code, the documentation and the knowledge of how it works. There is no lock-in to Essera.

Do you work with our existing systems?

Yes. Most of our work connects to software a business already runs on, so data moves between systems instead of being retyped by hand.

When is AI the wrong answer?

Often. If a simple integration or a rule-based workflow solves the problem reliably, that is what we recommend. We use AI where it measurably earns its place, such as reading documents or answering questions over your own files.

Where are you based?

Essera is based in the Research Triangle park in North Carolina and works with companies across the US, remotely and on site when it helps.
